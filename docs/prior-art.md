## What the pattern says

- Stranger identification by face won novelty prizes ("Most Wacky", "Chaotic Evil"). Main-track wins went to memory-aid and coaching framings. Lead with the coach and the follow-through.
- No project page carries consent language for the other party. Ours does, on the lens.
- No project says "no public info found" instead of guessing. Say it out loud.

## Open ground Cue holds (no prior project found with any of these)

1. Voice-named lookup on camera-less glasses, a cited public source per card, no face recognition.
2. "No public info found" as a first-class outcome, plus a decision gate that refuses cards about a stranger.
3. A wearer-only spoken debrief instead of recording the other person; recording only after an on-screen consent tap.
4. Delivery over iMessage, to the wearer only.
5. Proposed times with a tentative calendar hold after a conversation.
6. End-of-day and nightly coaching across conversations, with memory across days (the CREAO Coach on the journal).
7. The only Even G2 project built on the Even Hub SDK with a verified placement is none so far; G1 wins went through Mentra. The Even Hub track is open ground.
