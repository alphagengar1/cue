# Cue - 2-minute demo video script

Target length: 2:00 (hard cap 2:10). The glasses are on screen by 0:02 and the first card is on screen by about 0:08. Placeholders in [SQUARE BRACKETS] need real values. Voiceover is written at about 2.5 words per second; every beat fits its slot with a little air.

The demo person is David Cramer (Sentry, co-founder), who is in the preloaded event and has a public footprint. Keep the same person through the whole video so the debrief, the follow-up, the times and the coach all point at one brief.

## Capture setup

- **Through-the-lens shots:** a phone or camera held behind the right lens of the glasses, or a lens-capture rig. Dark background, glasses at a natural angle. If the lens is hard to film, use the mirror (below) as the main view and cut to through-the-lens for the first card and for Quiet.
- **Phone/laptop mirror:** the same `Screen` the HUD shows, rendered as HTML (the phone companion page inside the Even app, or the setup page's Judge view at https://35.164.205.54.sslip.io/setup?view=mirror). Use this for readable close-ups.
- **Simulator:** the Even Hub desktop simulator for any state-machine shot the live lens cannot give on camera. Label it "simulator" on screen so we stay honest.
- **Phone:** screen-record the iMessage thread on the wearer's phone (or film it over the shoulder). Hide the contact header and the sender number; the Photon line is not something we print.
- **Laptop:** two tabs ready before filming: agent.creao.ai with the Sessions tab open, and Google Calendar on the wearer's own calendar. Optional third tab: the Sentry trace for one lookup ([SENTRY TRACE LINK]).
- **Wide shot:** the teammate wearing the glasses, a consenting person across the table (a sponsor rep or friend with a public bio), real venue noise.
- Record voiceover separately. Burn in captions. No music louder than a bed track. Mute notification sounds on the laptop; leave them on for the phone, the buzz is part of the story.

## Notes for the edit

- The first 10 seconds must show the lens, not slides. If the live capture fails, use the simulator and caption it "simulator".
- Show real runs in the CREAO Sessions tab, not a settings page or the agent builder. The runs in the list must be the ones that produced the texts on the phone in this video, with matching timestamps. Open one run and show the JSON block at the end of the reply; that is what the backend reads. The Research agent's event run is from the setup page before the event; it can sit in the list but is not the one to open.
- The 90-second rewrite: the lookup happens at about 0:05 of footage, so the `★ updated` marker (Brief v2, the Researcher's facts) lands around 1:35 of real time; the Research agent's v3 follows a minute or two later and shows the same marker. Keep the lens camera rolling after the lookup and grab the `★ updated` foot whichever time it appears, then place it as the cut-back inside the 1:27 beat. If it does not fit, cut the cut-back and give the 2 s to the JSON close-up; the voiceover still reads correctly.
- The honesty line stays visible somewhere (end card or video description): "Person lookup is real web search. The demo event is preloaded with people who have public bios. The instant cards are Claude Haiku in about 3 s; the CREAO agents run for about 80 seconds to a few minutes and their results arrive on the phone and on the lens as they finish."
- What is verified and what is not, as of Oct 18 early. Say it in the description, do not hide it and do not overstate it:
  - Verified live from the backend: the fast path (lookup to cards in about 3 s), the Research agent rewrite (Brief v2, then v3 with `improvesOn`), debrief -> instant recap -> Follow-up agent (each texted by Photon as it completes), the Coach by texted question and after every third debrief, real Photon sends to the wearer's phone, the Follow-up agent's connector hold on the wearer's own calendar.
  - Verified on the physical G2 on Oct 18 (about 10:00): spoken lookup with a misheard name resolved, spoken debrief with the recap texted in seconds, "how did I do today?" starting the coach, a spoken goal change (even "cofinder"), and Record conversation (consent screen, two 20-second chunks transcribed).   - Fallback for filming if the spoken debrief misfires on the G2: send the same sentence to `POST /api/say` with the curl line in docs/pitch.md under "If something fails" (the setup page has a typed lookup box but no debrief box; the token never goes on screen), show the lens line from the simulator captioned "simulator", and keep the phone footage, which is real either way.
